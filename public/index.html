Before you start

Please confirm the following.

  • I will only record or enter information about a client when I am allowed to, and I will follow my firm's rules and the client's consent requirements.
  • Dictation uses my browser's speech service, which may send audio to that browser's provider. If that is not allowed for my work, I will type my notes instead.
  • The text I enter is processed by an AI service to write a summary. Audio and full notes are not stored by this app. Only the records I approve are saved to my account.
  • AI summaries can be wrong. I will review every record before approving it.
